# Trakt Month Calendar privacy policy

Effective date: 15 October 2026  
Publisher: eqagunn  
Applies to: Trakt Month Calendar

## What the extension processes

Trakt Month Calendar reads the content and controls of Trakt pages in your browser to arrange Trakt Calendar and Discover → Releases into a monthly view. This includes dates, titles, episode and season labels, image elements and the native controls and account-specific information displayed on those cards. It reads the current Trakt page URL to identify the active section.

The extension operates on `https://app.trakt.tv/`, `https://trakt.tv/` and `https://www.trakt.tv/`. It uses the website's own navigation and scrolling to load calendar periods. Card actions, including watched controls and information drawers, continue to use Trakt's native functionality.

## Where information goes

This processing takes place locally in your browser. The extension does not send page content or account information to the publisher or any other service operated by the publisher. It contains no analytics, advertising, telemetry or custom network-request code, and it does not sell or share user data.

Trakt and its image providers may receive the normal website requests caused by loading pages, calendar periods, images or using native card actions. Those requests are handled by the Trakt website and are governed by its own privacy policy. The extension does not replace or bypass your Trakt account permissions.

## Storage and retention

The extension keeps calendar references and loading state temporarily in the page’s memory. Reloading or closing the page discards that state. It does not save calendar content, account information or browsing history to persistent storage.

The four Auto/Manual preferences for Month View and Jump to Current Week in Calendar and Releases are saved in the browser’s local extension storage. These preferences remain after closing the page or restarting the browser, are not synced by the extension, and are not transmitted to the publisher. They can be changed from the extension popup and are removed when the extension is uninstalled. Trakt’s own website storage and browser HTTP caching remain governed by the website and browser.

## Your control

You can choose Auto or Manual for each action from the extension popup, or switch to Original View on the page. You can disable or uninstall the extension through your browser's extension settings to stop it running. Uninstalling it does not modify or delete your Trakt account or data held by Trakt.
